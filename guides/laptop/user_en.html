Delivery address

Tips & troubleshooting

Stuck?

01Company network blocks the portal?

Switch to your own 4G/5G and try again.

02Forgot your password?

Email the admin team shown on the sign-in screen.

03Wrong amount or ID?

You cannot edit after paying. Contact the PM in charge.

04Want another item?

Cancel reservation in box 03 before paying, then choose again.
